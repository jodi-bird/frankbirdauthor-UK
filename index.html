Water Water Everywhere...

Water is everywhere.

It sustains us, surrounds us and passes almost unnoticed through every part of modern life. Until something makes us question it.

What happens when the one thing nobody can live without becomes the one thing nobody can trust?

Water Water Everywhere... is a contemporary thriller spanning countries, institutions and lives, as an apparently disconnected series of events begins to reveal something far more disturbing.

About the author

Frank Bird

Frank Bird came to writing after a working life that repeatedly placed him close to technology, engineering, industry and the people responsible for making complicated things actually work.

Those experiences — together with a longstanding fascination with how apparently separate events can become connected — helped provide the foundations for Water Water Everywhere...

It is his first novel.
