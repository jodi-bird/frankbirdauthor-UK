Darren

Cold rain. Loud music. An empty stomach. And a decision that will connect an ordinary Northumberland morning to something far larger.

Darren walks the streets of Bellingham isolated behind oversized headphones and largely detached from the world around him.

What happens next appears, at first, to be little more than a piece of opportunistic petty crime. It isn't.
