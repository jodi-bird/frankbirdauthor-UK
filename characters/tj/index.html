'TJ'

'TJ' is a member of a Special Forces unit deployed to Afghanistan.

His experience in high-risk environments, tactical expertise and leadership skills make him an invaluable asset in the field.

In the story

In Afghanistan, 'TJ' is deployed with a Special Forces team operating in an unforgiving environment where patience, observation and precision are essential.

Working alongside Pete, Matt — known as Ali — and Jake, he is part of a close-knit team whose humour and friendship provide a rare contrast to the circumstances surrounding them.

For 'TJ', however, one particular assignment will connect events in Afghanistan with a story developing far beyond the compound walls.
