A little about me

Frank Bird

Proud to be Welsh, I still work as a project manager, and am delighted to have published my first novel, Water Water Everywhere…

Working life began as an apprentice carpenter and joiner, before I moved on to work as a ceramic tiler and concrete floor installer. A knee injury forced me to find a factory job, which led me to the gaming machine industry and a production-line role at JPM. Over the years, I took on further challenges in testing, development, customer service and training, before moving into new product development and eventually the casino industry. Along the way, I also spent a few years as managing director in the automotive industry supply chain but that is a story for another time.

These experiences all opened doors to places I could only have imagined as a tradesman. My work took me across Europe, Africa and eventually to the United States, where I had the opportunity to meet people whose lives were very different from my own.

In a later role at an organisation that headhunted me, I worked on mobile and online gaming, water treatment plants, and technology for police and armed forces. It was while working on the latter that the idea for Water Water Everywhere… began to take shape. Writing gave me a way to explore that idea, sparked by a simple essay for my daughter’s homework.

I found I could draw on places I’d visited, ideas I’d explored, and people I’d met, then let my imagination take the story in a new direction. My diaries and the accounts I’d written of my own life came in handy when I wanted to check what technology would have been available at the time.

Away from work, I enjoy walking, painting watercolours and playing backgammon. I’ve also tried surf kayaking, coasteering and learning the tenor saxophone—with varying degrees of success!

Publishing a novel has brought some wonderful new pleasures. Hearing that someone has enjoyed my creation is one of them, and having a colleague bring his copy to work for me to sign is another. After so many years of working on other people’s products, projects and misguided ideas under their direction and control, it feels especially comforting to hold my book with my own name on it.

I’m now working on a follow-up that explores the life behind one of the events mentioned in Water Water Everywhere…
