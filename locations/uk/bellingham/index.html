Bellingham

Bellingham, Northumberland
06:30 · Friday, 26 July 2013

Bellingham is a beautiful rural village in Northumberland, not far from the Scottish border.

Surrounded by dense forests and relatively isolated countryside, it has a close-knit community and lies within reach of some spectacular scenery, including Kielder Water.

Featured character

Darren

In this quiet Northumberland village of Bellingham, an ordinary Friday morning begins beneath a low, wet sky.

For Darren, the day starts as countless others have — black instant coffee, a routine patrol of the streets, and the familiar, unwelcoming surroundings of the town he calls home.

But as he slinks through the rain, looking for a chance to strike, he spots a likely target.

His decision to investigate it will trigger a strange chain of events for everyone who becomes involved.
