Sweden

Science and discovery

In Sweden, questions raised thousands of miles away begin to move from observation into science.

Laboratories, controlled testing and careful analysis offer the possibility of answers — but each answer begins to raise new and more troubling questions.

What began as an unexplained environmental event is becoming something measurable, repeatable and increasingly difficult to dismiss.

Featured character

Josie

Josie's work in Sweden moves the investigation into a new phase, where observation gives way to controlled scientific testing.

As the evidence develops, so does the risk — both to the investigation and to Josie herself.
