Helsinki

Finland
Northern Europe

Helsinki should represent safety, distance and a chance to disappear. For Dmitri, things are not quite that simple.

Having travelled a long way from the world he knows, Dmitri reaches Finland believing that crossing a border may also have put danger behind him.

In the quiet normality of Helsinki, ordinary routines begin to return. But events elsewhere have already placed him inside a story whose connections extend much further than he realises.

Featured character

Dmitri

Dmitri is a wealthy Russian businessman whose fortune was built through petrochemicals, investment and share trading during a remarkable period of change.

He is accustomed to wealth, influence and control — until a conversation with a trusted friend gives him reason to reconsider just how secure his world really is.

By the time he reaches Helsinki, Dmitri has already made some very significant decisions.
