Africa

Where the story begins

Far from laboratories, government buildings and cities, the first signs that something is wrong appear in a remote African landscape.

Here, isolated communities live alongside a harsh and beautiful environment where water is precious and survival has always depended upon understanding the land.

When wildlife begins to die without explanation, what first appears to be a local mystery becomes the beginning of something much larger.

Featured character

Josie

Josie arrives as part of a field team expecting difficult conditions, long days and the usual frustrations of working far from home.

What she encounters instead will place her at the centre of a mystery whose consequences will eventually reach far beyond Africa.

Gregoire

Gregoire knows this world in a way the visiting team cannot. The people, the landscape and the traditions surrounding them are familiar territory.
