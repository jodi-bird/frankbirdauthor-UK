Germany

Rhineland-Palatinate
Saturday · 27 July 2013

In Rhineland-Palatinate, an investigation already underway begins to reveal another piece of a much larger puzzle.

Severe summer storms, hail and flooded roads make an already difficult operation even more complicated as a police field team investigates suspected criminal activity.

What appears at first to be a local case involving drugs, illegal immigration and people trafficking will eventually connect with events unfolding far beyond Germany.

Featured character

Dieter

Dieter is meticulous, methodical and happiest when everything is precisely where it should be.

Unfortunately for him, the weather, his second vehicle and one particularly difficult morning have other ideas.

Despite circumstances that offend almost every instinct for order he possesses, Dieter eventually gets the job done.
